About Andromeda

Rigorous analysis made useful.

Andromeda Data Group is a Maryland-based political data and analytics company focused on helping progressive campaigns and organizations make clearer decisions from the information they already have—and the questions they still need to answer.

Our mission

Help campaign teams direct attention and resources where they can matter most.

Campaigns generate more data than most teams have time to interpret. Andromeda exists to close the distance between information and action.

We begin with the strategic decision—not a favorite model or a generic dashboard. Then we build the analysis, audience view or measurement system needed to support that decision clearly and responsibly.

What guides the work

A practical standard for campaign intelligence.

Useful over impressive

An analysis earns its place when it helps a team choose, prioritize or learn—not when it simply looks sophisticated.

Transparent methods

Assumptions, limitations and evaluation should be understandable to the people responsible for acting on the result.

Responsible data practice

Access, use, retention and delivery should reflect the sensitivity of political and voter information.

The Andromeda approach

Clear from the first question to the final recommendation.
