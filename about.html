About

Progressive political strategy powered by evidence.

Andromeda Data Group is a political data and analytics company focused on progressive campaigns and organizations that want modern modeling, sharper audience intelligence and clearer decision-making without building a large internal data operation.

Our mission

Help progressive campaigns identify and prioritize the right voters to contact so they can spend money and field effort more efficiently.

We believe campaign analytics should be rigorous enough for a data scientist and clear enough for a campaign manager. The output is not a model for its own sake—it is a better decision.

What guides the work
A

Useful over flashy

Every analysis should connect to a real campaign choice.

B

Transparent methods

Models should be evaluated, documented and understandable.

C

Responsible data use

Access, retention and analysis practices should reflect the sensitivity of political data.
