Services

Focused analysis for the decisions in front of your campaign.

Engage Andromeda for a specific strategic question or combine services into a tailored intelligence program. Every scope is designed to produce clear, campaign-ready outputs—not analysis that sits on a shelf.

Modeling

Voter modeling & targeting

Identify where persuasion, support and turnout opportunity are most likely to exist.

We build and evaluate statistical or machine-learning scores around your campaign’s strategic question. Results are translated into understandable segments and usable universes for field, mail and paid media.

  • Persuasion and support scores
  • Turnout and contact priority
  • Model validation and documentation
  • Audience exports and refresh plans
Learning

Message & creative testing

Learn which arguments work, for whom and by how much.

We structure tests around the choices your team is actually weighing, then measure overall effect and meaningful differences across audiences. The result is a practical read on what to scale, revise or stop.

  • Survey and experiment design
  • Lift and subgroup analysis
  • Message and creative comparison
  • Decision-ready reporting
Operations

Field & targeting optimization

Direct limited contact capacity toward the highest-value opportunities.

We combine voter characteristics, contact history and field performance to identify where another door, call or text is most likely to matter—and where the current program needs adjustment.

  • Canvass and phone prioritization
  • Contactability analysis
  • Geographic opportunity mapping
  • Field benchmarks and follow-up logic
Context

Narrative & audience intelligence

Understand the issues, communities and public signals shaping your race.

We organize approved public information into a clearer map of topics, relationships and emerging conversations. This helps campaign teams interpret change without confusing volume for strategic importance.

  • Issue and narrative monitoring
  • Audience and entity mapping
  • Topic clustering
  • Strategic signal briefs
Measurement

Campaign performance systems

Create a consistent view of what your campaign is doing and what it is producing.

We define useful operating measures, align inconsistent source data and build reporting that connects activity to outcomes. Teams gain one shared language for performance and resource tradeoffs.

  • KPI and measurement design
  • Dashboard-ready data structures
  • Trend and variance analysis
  • Resource allocation insights
