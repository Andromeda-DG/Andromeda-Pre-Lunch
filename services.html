Services

Data science built around progressive campaign decisions.

Choose a focused project or combine services into a campaign intelligence program designed for progressive candidates, campaigns and aligned organizations.

Persuasion Score Modeling

Prioritize the voters most likely to move.

We build statistical and machine-learning scores using the data available to your campaign. Models can estimate persuasion potential, support likelihood, turnout propensity or contact priority.

  • Voter-level scoring and segmentation
  • Model validation and explainable drivers
  • Exportable universes for field and paid media
  • Refreshes as campaign data changes

Message & Ad Testing

Measure which arguments work, for whom, and by how much.

We structure tests around your campaign’s strategic questions and quantify response differences across target audiences.

  • Survey and experiment design
  • Lift and subgroup analysis
  • Creative/message comparison
  • Decision-ready reporting

Voter Targeting & Field Optimization

Make every contact attempt more valuable.

We combine voter characteristics with campaign contact history to identify where additional outreach is most likely to matter.

  • Canvass and phone universe prioritization
  • Contact and follow-up optimization
  • Geographic targeting
  • Field performance benchmarks

Audience Graph

Understand the public conversation around your race.

We organize public digital signals into an audience and issue map so campaigns can see how topics, communities and narratives relate.

  • Public-web and social signal collection
  • Topic and entity clustering
  • Audience relationship mapping
  • Emerging narrative monitoring

Campaign Performance Metrics

Connect activity to outcomes.

We turn canvassing, phonebank, fundraising and other operational data into a consistent measurement framework.

  • Campaign KPI design
  • Automated performance dashboards
  • Trend and variance analysis
  • Resource allocation insights
